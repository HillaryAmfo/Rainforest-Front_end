Our Agroforestry & Ecological Initiatives

Community-Based Tree Planting

Empowering local communities in the Greater Accra Region to plant indigenous trees, restoring degraded lands and enhancing biodiversity.

  • Focus on native species
  • Involves local farmers and youth
  • Improves soil health and water retention

Sustainable Agroforestry Farms

Promoting integrated farming systems that combine trees and crops, providing sustainable livelihoods for farmers and increasing food security.

  • Training in agroforestry techniques
  • Diversification of income sources
  • Climate-smart agriculture practices

Reforestation of Degraded Forest Reserves

Working to restore vital forest reserves in Ghana that have been impacted by deforestation, protecting critical habitats.

  • Seedling propagation of native trees
  • Partnerships with forestry agencies
  • Monitoring and conservation efforts
